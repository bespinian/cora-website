00 - THE STORY

Cora was built against something.

Every sovereign tool starts with a threat somebody took seriously. Cora's threat is called Omnivor - invented as a character, copied straight out of real contracts and real data centres.

THE ADVERSARY

Omnivor

A corporation with no address and a single business model: collect everything. Omnivor gives tools away - a plugin here, a free model there, one line of autocomplete - and sends every request home. Your code. Your client files. Your half-finished ideas. Its motto is in the small print: “We're already inside.”

THE QUIET INVASION

No country gets conquered. It gets migrated.

Omnivor never has to cross a border - a ticked checkbox in the terms will do. Laptop by laptop, repo by repo, department by department, Switzerland slides into an infrastructure nobody here operates, audits or can switch off. It never feels like an attack. It feels like onboarding.

THE BURN

What he doesn't pay for, he takes

Data is not the only thing Omnivor is hungry for. He builds where power is cheap and nobody asks questions, cools with water from rivers that already run too warm in summer, and parks diesel generators alongside when the grid cannot keep up. The waste heat goes into the sky. On his balance sheet it is a line of operating cost. On the ground it is the water table.

THE ANSWER

Cora holds the gate

Built in Bern, run in Zurich and Geneva - on a grid that is already among the cleanest in Europe. Cora works as fast as the corporation's tools, in the same terminal, in the same editor, except nothing leaves and she routes every task to the smallest model that can finish it. Every token stays on Swiss soil, every tool call lands in the log, every model lies open for inspection. What we do not claim: that any of it is free.
