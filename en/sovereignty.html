Three questions that settle it

Ask them of every provider on your list. Ours included.

Can you keep working if the provider disappears tomorrow?

With Cora, yes: the weights are Apache-2.0, the runtime ships as a Helm chart, and the escrow agreement hands you both.

Which foreign authority can compel disclosure?

None. bespinian has no US parent, no US subsidiary and no US sub-processors - the CLOUD Act has no addressee.

Where does capacity come from at peak?

From reserved Swiss GPUs. Requests queue instead of failing over abroad. It is written into the SLA.

Built from open source

Cora is not a black box. Your security team can read what runs on its machines instead of trusting a changelog.

  • Qwen-Coder and Apertus - open-weight models, Apache-2.0
  • vLLM - high-throughput inference serving
  • llm-d on Kubernetes - scheduling and routing, shipped as Helm charts

Plus a signed SBOM per release and a tested runbook that migrates a tenant into your own cluster in less than two weeks.
