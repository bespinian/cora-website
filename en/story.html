From character to feature

The story is invented; the counter-moves are not. Cache's staring, for instance, is the audit trail.

  • Open weights - no black box. You can read it and take it with you.
  • Swiss soil - no failover abroad, because there is none.
  • Redaction at the edge - secrets and personal data drop out before a model sees them.
  • Audit trail - every attempt becomes visible. Including the ones from inside.
  • Renewable power - hydro and solar off the country's own grid.
  • Fewer tokens - routing and caching instead of brute force.

What we do not claim

That any of this is free. That it is climate neutral. Running a model costs power, water and material, and no certificate changes that. We tell you where the power comes from, where the heat goes and how many tokens a task cost. You can do the arithmetic yourself - and that is exactly the difference to Omnivor.
