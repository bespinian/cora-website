THE STORY

Cora doesn't start out a hero. She starts out a bystander.

This is Cora's story: how she begins in a server room in a small city - no authority and no audience - follows the data all the way to a hall with no address, and comes back with exactly what she lacked there.

The decision underneath it is real: who do you hand your code, your client files and your half-finished ideas to - and which country is the machine in that they land on? Omnivor does not exist. The clauses and the data centres we built it from do.

FOUR ACTS24 PANELS
THE CAST
ACT I - THE GIFT

Cora sees it first. And can do nothing.

A takeover doesn't begin with a threat. It begins with a tool that works, costs nothing, and on a Tuesday morning takes exactly the job off your hands that you had no time for. Cora is already there when it happens. That is her problem.

ACT II - THE QUIET INVASION

So she follows the data

Omnivor never has to cross a border. A ticked checkbox in the terms will do. Cora wants to know where that leads - and takes Cache, who considers this a distinctly bad idea.

ACT III - WHAT IT DOESN'T PAY FOR

On the way back she takes the detour

A model this size is hungry for more than data - for power, water and landscape. Cora wants to see where that comes from. Cache would rather not.

ACT IV - THE GATE

Cora builds the gate she didn't have

She doesn't come back a hero, she comes back with a list. Everything she couldn't do out there gets built now: her own models, her own inference server, and a gate in front of it that somebody with a name has to open. And a desk of his own for Cache.

FROM CHARACTER TO FEATURE

What Omnivor stands for - and what Cora puts against it

The story is invented, the counter-moves are not. Every act has a counterpart in the product - Cache's staring, for instance, is the audit trail. Four of them are about sovereignty, two about energy.

OPEN WEIGHTS

No black box. You can read what is thinking in there - and take it with you.

SWISS GROUND

No cross-border fallback, because there is none to fall back to.

REDACTION AT THE EDGE

Secrets and personal data drop out before any model sees them.

AUDIT TRAIL

Every attempt becomes visible. Including the ones from inside the house.

RENEWABLE POWER

Hydro and solar from the grid the data centres are plugged into.

FEWER TOKENS

Routing and caching instead of brute force. The greenest token is the one never generated.

WHAT WE DO NOT CLAIM

That any of it is free. That it is climate-neutral. Running a model costs power, water and material, and no certificate changes that. What we will tell you is where the power comes from, where the heat goes, and how many tokens a task cost. The rest you can work out yourself - and that is precisely the difference from Omnivor.
