Sustainability

Keera runs on Swiss power

Keera hangs off one of the cleanest grids in Europe: hydro and solar, the same power as the rest of the country. What we can show is here. So is what we cannot.

Where the power comes from

The grid

Swiss electricity is mostly hydro, with a fast-growing solar share. That is the grid Keera runs on.

The contract

We pick Swiss hosting partners with a renewable supply contract and name them in due diligence.

The hardware

We prefer sites with liquid cooling, low overhead and waste-heat reuse where it exists.

Fewer tokens

Routing and caching keep the token count low. The greenest token is the one never generated.

The story

Somebody else pays Omnivor's bill

In the story Keera is built against, there is a corporation called Omnivor. It is invented. What it does with power and water is not.

Omnivor

It builds where power is cheap and nobody asks questions, cools with river water that is already too warm in summer, and blows the waste heat into the air. The region pays for it.

Keera

Keera runs on the country's own grid, prefers sites that reuse waste heat, and sends each task to the smallest model that can do it. That does not make it climate neutral - it makes it checkable.

What we claim - and what we do not

Green promises are easy to make and hard to prove. Here is the line we hold.

What we say

  • Keera is hosted in Switzerland, on renewable power.
  • We pick partners on efficiency, not only on price.

What we do not say

  • That AI is climate neutral - or that offsets make it so.
  • Exact per-request figures we cannot yet measure.

What we are working on

  • Per-tenant energy reporting in the gateway's audit log.
  • A published methodology before we publish any number.
