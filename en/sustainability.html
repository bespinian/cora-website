WHERE THE POWER COMES FROM
01 THE GRID

Swiss hydro & solar

Swiss electricity is among the cleanest in Europe - mostly hydro, with solar growing fast. That is the grid Cora runs on.

02 THE CONTRACT

Renewable supply, on paper

We choose Swiss hosting partners on a renewable electricity contract, and we name them and their supply in due diligence.

03 THE HARDWARE

Chosen for efficiency

We favour sites with liquid cooling, low overhead, no evaporative water use and heat recovery where it exists.

04 THE DEMAND

Less compute per task

Clean power still costs the grid something. Routing and caching keep the number of tokens - and the energy behind them - down.

THE STORY

Somebody else pays Omnivor's bill

In the story Cora was built against, there is a corporation called Omnivor. It is invented. The way it treats power and water is not.

OMNIVOR

Take until there is nothing left

It builds where power is cheap and nobody asks questions. It cools with river water that already runs too warm in summer. It adds diesel generators when the grid cannot keep up, and blows the waste heat into the sky. None of it is in its climate brochure - and the region it lands in pays for it.

CORA

On the same grid as the country

Cora runs on Swiss hydro and solar, prefers halls with liquid cooling and waste-heat recovery, and routes every task to the smallest model that can finish it. That does not make her climate-neutral - it makes her checkable. What we do not conclude from it is written below.

What we claim, and what we don't

Green claims are easy to make and hard to verify. Here is the line we hold.

WE DO SAY
Cora is hosted in Switzerland, on renewable electricity.
We pick partners on efficiency and cooling, not just price.
Our product decisions aim to reduce compute per task.
WE DON'T SAY
That AI is carbon-neutral, or that offsets make it so.
Precise per-request figures we cannot yet measure end to end.
Numbers from a certification we haven't completed.
WE'RE WORKING ON
Energy reporting per tenant in the Gateway's audit record.
Naming supply and facility details in due diligence packs.
Publishing a methodology before publishing any metric.
EFFICIENCY BY DESIGN

The greenest token is the one you never generate

Sustainability is an engineering choice too. Cora routes work to the smallest model that can do it and caches aggressively - that cuts your bill and the load on the grid.

→Right-sized routing: small model first, escalate only when needed
→Prompt and context caching across a session and a team
→Batch and off-peak scheduling for large refactor jobs
→Token and compute usage visible per team in the Gateway
